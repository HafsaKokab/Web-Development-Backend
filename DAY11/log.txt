Semantic Versioning (SemVer)
4.18.3 → Version number
1st part (4) = Major → Major changes / breaking changes
2nd part (18) = Minor → New features, usually backward compatible
3rd part (3) = Patch → Minor bug fixes

Q: Why is the latest version not always recommended for projects?
Latest versions may introduce new bugs, breaking changes, or compatibility issues with your project.
A stable, tested version is safer and more reliable for production projects.
